Shows one of your Codex pets in Paseo. The pet acts out the most urgent state across the workspaces on every connected host: waiting when an agent needs your input, failed, working, ready to review, or idle.

On desktop and in the browser the pet floats over the window. Drag it anywhere; click it to open a list of the workspaces that need you, each with a button that opens it. On phones it lives in the sidebar footer.

Pets are read from `~/.codex/pets` and from Orca's custom pets folder on the machine running this plugin's daemon, using the Codex pet format (`pet.json` and a spritesheet). Pick a pet and its size on the Pet screen.
